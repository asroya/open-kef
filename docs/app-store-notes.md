- Onboarding of KEF wireless speakers to the network
- Streaming services: Spotify, TIDAL, Amazon Music, Qobuz, Deezer, HighResAudio, Internet Radio, Podcasts (no Apple Music)
- Playback control, volume, input source selection
- Speaker sound settings (EQ / room), sleep timer, auto-wake source, child lock

## Notes for RE
- The Mac build is the iPad app running on Apple silicon (not a native Mac or Catalyst build), so pulling it from a Mac gives the iOS binary (`/Applications/KEF Connect.app/Wrapper/...`), which is likely encrypted (FairPlay). We may need a decrypted copy or to rely on network traffic capture instead.
- Recent user reviews mention AirPlay unreliability and Wi-Fi breakage after updates; connectivity/reliability is a real differentiator.
